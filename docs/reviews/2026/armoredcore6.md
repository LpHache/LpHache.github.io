**Armored Core VI** is a single player action Mech game developped by From Software. You play as augmented human C4-621, an independant mercenary deployed to the planet Rubicon taking any contract you can (at first) and following your handler's intructions. The game is played as a series of fixed missions, divided into 5 chapters, where you return to your arsenal in between each of them. Your arsenal is where you buy new mech parts, customize your mech, do practice runs, etc. You can replay any mission you've done before to get a ranking (D to S - Rank) and farm credits. The missions are very varied with some cool boss fights, good story moments, and 3 alternate endings.<br/>

#### **Final Opinion**
This game was honestly so much fun. It is right up my alley and hits all the spots I wanted it to hit. Big weapons with big impact, big mechs, cool effets, epic fights, cheesy one liners, grand finale... it had it all. The mechs felt good to pilot and were reactive, the customization options were vast (and not just minor damage tweaks) and they looked sick as hell. While some might find replaying the game 3 times to get all the endings a bit tiresome, I did not mind the excuse to bust out new mech designs and keep enjoying this game over and over again. The boss fights were honestly so good and the ice worm fight in particular, while not mechanically demanding, was just so *cool*. Its also refreshing to have a good action game thats not open world or anything and you just go from mission to mission pretty quickly. I almost forgot to mention the story actually hits kinda hard? I did not expect to make such hard decision and feel so many emotions in my mecha fighting game. If you like mechs at all, I highly recommend playing this game.<br/>

#### **The Good**
**++++ There are 306 mech parts** to make a unique mech that you will enjoy. This includes 103 unique weapons from lazer drills to  bazookas to homing missiles to shields, and 4 different leg frames that change the way you pilot the mech completely (bipedal, reverse jointed, quadruped, tank)<br/>
**++++ The gameplay is fast, reactive, challenging and so fun.** That is all.<br/>
**+++ The story is actually super good.** I did not expect much coming in, and left super satisfied. All the endings were different enough from each other (not just a different cutscene, it was multiple missions and multiple bosses of difference)<br/>
**+++ The dialogue is lowkey great.** *I won't miss - Bwooooooooooooomshhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh*, *So tell me Buddy, who needs you ?*<br/>
**+++ Loved the boss fights.** They were challenging, they were all pretty varied, and the aura farming was insane. Fun to make a build that works when you hit a wall too.<br/>
**++ The arena mode is dope.** It lets you fight nearly every enemy AC in the game 1v1 with no health packs.<br/>

#### **The Bad**
**- Some might consider requiring 3 full playthroughs** (can't save scum here) to see all endings to be a bit tedious and annoying (but to me its just more to play!)<br/>
**- The environments** do get a bit one-note with everything being either ice, or brutalist military architecture. All a mix of white, grey, brown and black.<br/>
**- The last achievement** to get 100% is getting S-Rank on every single mission (59 total). That is *actually* tedious.<br/>
