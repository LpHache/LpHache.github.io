**Ball X Pit** is a fun take on the brick breaking type of game with survival roguelite elements. The main gameplay loop involves shooting balls at rows of slowly advancing enemies that hurt once they reach you. Each level has 2 mini bosses and 1 final boss to clear the area.  Your character shoots special balls which all have effects and baby balls which just do regular damage. You can upgrade, fusion, and evolve these balls over the course of a ru nas well as accumulate passives. In between runs you build out a base that gives various upgrades which will make you stronger during the runs.<br/>

#### **Final Opinion**
Ball X Pit was a pretty enjoyable game overall. It kept its mechanics pretty simple but the loop was addicting and it was great seeing builds come together. My favorite part was for sure completing the encyclopedia and finding every evolution possible. The base building and ressource management in between did its job fine to scale up in power. I did find that by the end I was so strong it almost didn't matter what balls I went, just about any combination was strong enough to clear the levels which was a bit unfortunate. Would have been fun if later levels had a bit more skill challenges involved.<br/>

#### **The Good**
**++++ Refreshing take on a unique genre.** I've always loved brick breakers as a kid playing on my parent's phone and stuff. It's fun to see a new take on it that really pushes the mechanics to it's limits.<br/>
**++++ Great builds.** They make a lot of ball evolutions that were really fun to find and make builds around.<br/>
**+++ Good character choices.** With 18 characters, all with a different perk that you could combine with others, it made you rethink how your build would interact with the perk during every run.<br/>
**++ Good roguelite mechanics.** The progression of building upgrades in between runs went at a good pace and felt meaningfull to my runs.<br/>
**+ Getting all achievements wasn't too hard.** A bit of a grind, but not hard to do.<br/>

#### **The Bad**
**-- Levels were all too samey.** From the first to the last, all levels felt about the same to me besides the color palette and enemy models. Sure, the ground hazard changed, and maybe some enemies had a different projectile, but otherwise it all played relatively the same.<br/>
**- Mini bosses did nothing.** They made mini bosses but most of them were just normal mobs with a bigger health pool. I wish they had given them some mechanic to play around or something.<br/>
**- Once I finished the encyclopedia, I had nothing left to play for.** While I was finishing up all the levels with every character, what motivated me was finding and unlocking all evolutions. Once I finished that, it exposed how OP I had become and how easy runs had become.<br/>
