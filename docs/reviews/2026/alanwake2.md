**Alan Wake 2** is a third person narrative driven horror game that continues the story of Alan Wake and his fight against the Dark Place. A ritual murder brings a new face from the FBI, Saga Anderson, to Bright Falls where famous writer Alan Wake had disappeared many years prior. In this game you will be able to switch between Saga and Alan as they each fight to restore reality to some semblance of normal, and save the people of Bright Falls. For Saga, you will explore the cauldron lake area, Bright Falls, and the small town of watery where Coffee World is set up. Her goal is to solve the case of the ritual murder, investigate cult activity, and bring Alan Wake back to rewrite the ending of the horror story. On the Alan side, you are stuck in the Dark Place, an obscure version of New York. You follow clues and change details of the story to help Saga on the other side and eventually escape from the Dark Place as well. Both characters play pretty differently and they provide a fun change of page at regular intervals. Each characters gets their own weapons and upgrades, and well as different gameplay mechanics. <br/>

#### **Final Opinion**
I thought Alan Wake 2 was a fantastic games. It had been a while since I had played a game with such a well written story with great characters. I loved all the new mechanics they introduced compared to the original game and it really gave it a ton of extra depth. I loved how immersive the game was and how invested it made me with it's great story. I may have started off slow but I  really blew through the back half of the game super quickly because of how much fun I was having. The enemy variety wasn't huge but you had tons of different tools to fight and I never really got bored of it either. I appreciate how natural all the characters behaved and how well written the dialogue was between them. The horror elements were just right (besides the jump scares) and gave the game a great atmosphere. I also enjoyed all the Control references, which played a major part to be honest, with the Federal Bureau of Control and how it all integrates into this larger world. All in all, a fantastic game that I would really recommend if you love focused story driven games and can handle a little bit of spooky elements. I have a few minor gripes with it, but it doesn't change how great of a game this was.<br/>

#### **The Good**
**++++ Investigation elements for both characters were super fun to interact with and unique in their execution.** Saga has the Mind Place, where she goes in her head and pieces together all the clues of all the active mysteries. As a player you have to fit each clue at the right place to make deductions or make new threads. It's done in a perfect way to not be tedious, but remain interactive and visually interesting. For Alan, he gets to rewrite scenes to fit new scenarios which change the map around him and opens new paths on the map. There's usually 4-5 places per chapter that have a scenes and its really cool to imagine the same area with a bunch of different stories in them.<br/>
**++++ The story was really amazing.** I didn't take long for me to be really invested in the story and it's characters. The locations are detailed, the dialogue isn't cringey, the characters are good, the mystery is fun to unravel. <br/>
**+++ NPCs behave naturally.** It's refreshing to have dialogue and cutscenes where characters move around, pick things up, and overall act more human. So much better than so many RPG's that still just do still face shots that go back and forth. Mostly talking about major NPCs here and not random civilians.<br/>
**+++ Action felt good.** The guns for both characters all had good reasons to be used and felt different. Some of the upgrades also changed them quite a bit. Combat was always interactive between flashlight boosts, shooting weakspots, using flares, and fashbangs. Never felt powerless which is good to keep the game not too scary.<br/>
**+++ Saga had good optional content.** I thought finding all the caches and nursery rhyme puzzle locations was cool. The rewards were worth the little extra exploring it demanded, plus the rhymes were creepy and mysterious.<br/>
**+++ The settings were well crafted.** Almost each chapter had it own crafted area that was full of detail, interesting to explore, and set a good mood for the story.<br/>
**+++ The music is full of bangers.** They bring back the band from Control, and created a bunch more music for the game. Enjoyed it a lot.<br/>

#### **The Bad**
**- The use of jump scares was a bit too frequent.** Which is a bit of a shame since the game actually has great spooky atmoshpere and didn't really need the extra scares to work.<br/>
**- I did encounter 1 bug that made me have to restart and lose a little progress.** Always unfortunate when that happens.<br/>
**- They added extra stuff to the end cutscene in NG+**. I understand wanting to give people a reason to re-play it (since NG+ was added after launch), but imo that should just be extra lore and fun discoveries, not a major change to the end. The game was great but once you've solved the story there's little to motivate me to re-play the game, unlike a heavy action game like AC6 that is fun even if you know the story since the combat carries it.<br/>
