Returnal

Score: 9/10

Playtime: 26 hours
Platform: PC
Storefront: Steam







Returnal is a third person shooter rogue-like bullet hell game where you play as Selene, an astronaut, stranded on the mysterious planet Atropos. Your motivations in each biome differ as you search for the reasons why you crashed on the planet and why you keep repeating the cycle. Each biome has a randomly generated assortment of rooms with a few optional paths but nothing to get lost in. There is one main path that is clearly marked that keeps you going towards the end of the biome. Rooms either require clearing out all the enemies, or are bonus rooms with weapons, health and other pickups. Any death during your cycle brings you back to the beginning of biome 1 (or 4). You must clear biome 1 to 3 in one cycle for Act 1 ending, biome 4 to 6 in one cycle for Act 2 ending. Very little carries over between cycles except a few permanent traversal upgrades and your skill unlike other rogue likes that have more progression systems. However, there are a lot of progression system within each run with weapon levels, artifacts, suit upgrades, parasites and health upgrades making you very powerful by the time you reach the 3rd biome usually. Most biomes have a boss battle to win before proceeding to the next. Once you've beaten a boss once though, you often unlock shortcuts to bypass it for future runs. The game also has an endless mode with multiplayer leaderboards.

Final Opinion

I absolutely love the gameplay loop of this game. It just worked so well and the pace was really good. Combat was just really well executed in my opinion. It was fast paced, challenging, varied and with good map layouts. It kind of reminded me of Doom which I played right before but in third person with how good the combat was, and the way you tackle each encounter. I liked how it rewarded changing weapons all the time by unlocking permanent traits for each, and the decisions related to equipping parasites (they have negative conditions). I'm not sure how to explain it but there was just a flow to the whole game that just meshed with me really well. I wasn't a huge fan of the story however. It's not that it was bad but it just wasn't really what I expected or would have liked for this kind of game. Some of the story segments were also slow first person segments that kind of took you out of flow. They were only 5 mins and theres only lik 6 of them, but I still just wanted them to be over asap. Overall, I'd easily recommend returnal. The gameplay was tighter than I expected, and if you don't mind roguelike games it's not too long to finish either.

The Good

++++ Combat was fluid and fun. Gunplay and movement were downright great in this game. Lots of cool weapons, some consumables and special attacks to try. The bullet hell elements were very fair in my opinion and with the dash there was always a way to dodge all the enemy projectiles (of which there are a lot of).
++++ Replayability was good. Never felt like each run was too samey. There are a lot of modifiers you acquire that make each run pretty unique.
+++ Enemy and boss design was cool. Lots of different enemies in each biome that were honestly super fun to fight. The bosses were fun and cinamtic to fight, but stayed surprisingly fair and I never died much to bosses.
+++ Map design and environments were really good. Lots of elevation changes, grappling points, and cover in most arenas to reward quick movement and repositioning. I also think the biomes were pretty cool in general.
++ Quick load in. Opening the game brings you right back where you were in your cycle. There is no main menu at all so you just keep going super quickly I love it.

The Bad

-- I was not a huge fan of the main story. MAJOR SPOILERS: Basically, the whole thing is about dealing with trauma and internal conflict about the protagonist's past. The thing is, it's done well enough, but I kinda of just wanted it to be an actualy planet full of cool deadly aliens and constructs you know ? Because it starts off by presenting it like that's what it is until you delve further into the biomes.
- Unfortunate that you can't pause a run in the endless mode. I think because of multiplayer leaderboards, to prevent score manipulation with quiting and re-loading in, they just disabled the option to suspend a run in this mode which is really unfortunate. I just played it for the extra lore and fights and for fun, and it sucks to have to end the run if I want to quit the game. Especially since it's an endless mode that could go on for a long time.
